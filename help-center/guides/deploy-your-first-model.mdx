## Quick Tutorial

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload Your Dataset">
    Log in to the dashboard. Go to the Datasets tab and click "Upload".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Train a Model">
    Choose your dataset, select a model type, and click "Train".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy to Production">
    Once training is completed, click "Deploy" next to the trained model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Inference Endpoint">
    Copy the REST endpoint URL for your application to start sending requests.
  </Step>
</Steps>
