## Quick Start

1. Sign up at `https://dashboard.percepteye.ai`
2. Confirm your email address
3. Log in using your chosen credentials
4. Create or join a team workspace
5. Use the onboarding guide to configure your first project

## Accessing the Dashboard

Go to `https://dashboard.percepteye.ai` to view analytics, manage datasets, or deploy models.

## Need more help?
Contact support anytime at `support@percepteye.ai`.
