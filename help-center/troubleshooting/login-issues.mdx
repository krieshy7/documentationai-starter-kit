---
title: Unable to Log In
description: Fix login problems quickly.
---

## Symptoms
- Can't access dashboard
- Error: "Invalid credentials" or "Account not found"

## Fixes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Credentials">
    Make sure your email and password are correct. Reset if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset Password">
    Click "Forgot password?" on the login page and follow the steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email">
    Look for the confirmation email (check spam), and follow the link.
  </Step>
</Steps>
